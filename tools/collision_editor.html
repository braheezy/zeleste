Solid, one-way, and spike modes: click or drag to mark tiles. Spring mode: click a pixel position for a 14px spring. Strawberry and Dash Refill modes: click the center point. Stone Platform mode: click or drag 8x8 blocks. Mech Block mode: drag an 8px-grid start rectangle, then Shift-click or Shift-drag the target center. Right click or hold Option/Alt to erase. Spawn mode: click to add respawn points. Falling Block mode: drag a rectangle around the block tiles, click a block to select it. Shift-click sets the selected block's landing row. Foreground Stamp mode: choose a stamp, click to place, right click or Option-click to erase. Bridge poles are placed as foreground stamps. Bird NPC mode: click for bird origin, Shift-click for hint bubble origin, right click or Option-click to clear.
